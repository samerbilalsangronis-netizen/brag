  ⚠ No se pudo confirmar el dato de hoy (22 de octubre); una sola fuente da 194.000.
  Fuentes: [Babypips](https://www.babypips.com/news/headline-us-initial-jobless-claims-layoffs-2026-10-09), [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/jobless-claims/news/592380), [TheStreet](https://pro.thestreet.com/market-commentary/initial-jobless-claims-extend-longest-run-since-1969)
